# HotaruNet Privacy Policy

🌐 **Language:** **English** | [🇮🇷 فارسی (Persian)](PRIVACY.fa.md)

**Effective Date:** 2026

HotaruNet is designed to protect user privacy and freedom of access.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- HotaruNet does **not** collect browsing history, DNS queries, destination websites, IP addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, or device hardware identifiers.
- VPN traffic is routed directly between your device and tunnel endpoints without inspection or logging by the application.

## Anonymous Telemetry
- To display the aggregate live online users indicator inside the app, HotaruNet sends only a randomly generated local installation UUID (`client_id`) and aggregate byte counters without any personal or traffic content metadata.

## Update Checks
- On launch, the app queries the public GitHub Releases API for `OneForAllHub/HotaruNet` solely to notify you if a newer version is published.
